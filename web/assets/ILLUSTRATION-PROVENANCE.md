## Shared art direction

Use case: illustration-story. Premium hand-painted 3D/isometric editorial cartoon; tactile matte enamel, rounded mechanical detail, soft studio daylight and subtle paper grain. Friendly cream-and-terracotta robot technicians care for recognizable teal GPU hardware. Palette: cream `#fff8ec`, deep blue-teal `#173d49`, terracotta `#db704b`, golden yellow `#e7ba4a`, soft mint `#9dd6bc`. Wide landscape composition. No branding, logos, letters, numbers, watermark, flowchart arrows or internal-system claims.

## Final prompt set

1. **Fleet observatory:** A warm isometric cutaway datacenter with three dark-teal GPU racks, vented accelerator blades, lime status lights, aqua cooling pipes and heat exchanger, coral/gold optical cables, one small robot inspecting an open rack, a second telescope-like observatory robot with abstract waveform screen, telemetry dots and a foreground maintenance cart with GPU boards. Warm cream margin, distinct fully framed objects and calm workshop atmosphere.
2. **Burn-in laboratory:** A substantial GPU server on a wheeled bench inside a transparent stress-test chamber; visible accelerator cards, cable harness, fan and cooling hose; abstract instrument waveforms. A qualified teal rack on the left with a small robot holding a green check paddle. A separate withheld node in a coral-railed maintenance bay on the right with amber light and unplugged cord. Central chamber dominates one coherent workshop scene.
3. **Optic repair journey:** Three adjacent workshop scenes. Left: linked GPU racks, amber faulty transceiver and robot observing jagged waveform. Center: technician robot removes an optical transceiver and holds a fresh spare above a tray. Right: healthy synchronized racks, green lights, verification robot holding green check paddle and clean aligned waveforms. Continuous cream workshop environment, no arrows or captions, no fire or sparks.

## Animation guidance

Keep the raster illustration itself intact. In the website, overlay a small number of CSS/SVG particles on the hero's cable path, a soft status-light pulse, and a restrained scan highlight. For the repair journey, move a focus ring between the left, center and right thirds as the real simulator progresses through diagnosis, repair and verification. Expose state in accessible HTML text and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Do not use animation to imply a real system event unless it is driven by the simulator or clearly labelled as decorative.

The published WebP images are compressed exports of the original generated PNG artwork. Animation is implemented independently in CSS and SVG overlays.
